AI Assistant

Plan your project with a built-in AI assistant

The WebSphere AI Assistant helps you and your group before the project even starts. Generate ideas, vote on them together, and get practical guidance for planning the whole project.

Idea Management

Turn ideas into your next project

Everyone in the group can submit project ideas, review them together, and start a live vote to choose the idea your team will build.

Group Chat & Idea Voting

Talk it through and decide together

Each group has its own chat for communication and collaboration. Discuss your ideas with your teammates, then vote right beside the conversation to pick the project your team will work on.

Workflow Analytics

See how your team is really doing

A visual summary of your project progress and team performance, with weekly task completion, member performance, bottleneck analysis, and tool usage in one place.

Predictive Monitoring
